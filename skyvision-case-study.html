DaoAI World SkyVision is a no-code AI video surveillance platform that enables operators to build, train, and deploy custom visual AI models without writing a single line of code. All video processing runs on-premise with zero cloud dependency — data never leaves your facility. SkyVision supports up to 256 simultaneous camera streams with under 12ms inference latency, and is compatible with any RTSP/ONVIF camera, making it a drop-in upgrade for existing surveillance infrastructure.
